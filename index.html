June 2026 SNA Passenger Traffic

John Wayne Airport served 993,833 passengers in June 2026, a year-over-year (YoY) decrease of 2.8% versus June 2025 (1,022,397). Year-to-date 2026 traffic totals 5,515,352 passengers, up 1.4% versus YTD 2025.

June 2026 International Passenger Traffic

SNA's international passenger volume totaled 18,029 passengers in June 2026, a decrease of 29.2% versus June 2025. YTD 2026 international traffic of 112,355 passengers is down 26.2% versus YTD 2025.

June 2026 Aircraft Operations

Total aircraft operations (takeoffs and landings) were 25,028 in June 2026, an 11.5% decrease versus June 2025, driven by a 16.0% decline in general-aviation activity.

SNA Total Passenger Traffic — January 2021–June 2026

SNA's traffic has followed a clear recovery-and-plateau arc: from a pandemic trough of 7.7 million passengers in 2021, through a strong rebound to 11.4 million in 2022, to a series peak of 11.7 million in 2023. Volume pulled back 5.6% in 2024 (11.1 million) before recovering 2.5% in 2025 (11.4 million), nearly matching the 2023 high. 2026 has been more uneven: January–April ran 2%–6% ahead of the same months in 2025, but both May and June came in 2.8% behind their 2025 counterparts — a sign that momentum cooled heading into summer even as year-to-date volume (up 1.4%) still leads 2025.
